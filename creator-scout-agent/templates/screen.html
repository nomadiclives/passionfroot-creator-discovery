A check nobody answered is not a pass.

The spec asks for PASS / FLAG / FAIL per check. This screen adds a fourth answer — UNKNOWN — for checks where nothing was ever sourced. A triage that reported PASS on a creator nobody looked at would launder an absence of evidence into a clean bill of health, which is the same failure as inventing a metric, one level up: a judgement nobody made rather than a number nobody measured.

So an unanswered blocking check holds the whole row at NEEDS_SCREENING, and it outranks a FLAG — a concern somebody weighed is a better position than a gap nobody looked at.

What screening will not do

  • Infer demographics from content. "They mention dorms, so the audience is 18-24" is a guess wearing evidence's clothes. Content signals are gathered and shown; they are never written to audience_18_24_pct or geo_us_pct.
  • Score, rank or assign a role. Triage answers whether to consider someone at all. Judgement belongs on the judging screen, scoring to the engine.
  • Read a creator's last 60 days. Brand safety and the follower growth curve are human reads. No API in this app classifies controversy, and none returns a follower time series, so those checks stay UNKNOWN until a person records an answer on the row.
  • Treat an empty field as good news. A creator with no sourced sponsors has not been cleared of a competitor conflict — nobody looked.
